AI analysis platforms deliver strong synthesis with ephemeral output: nothing compounds, nothing is kept in a form an auditor could replay, every analysis starts from zero. Agent-governance platforms preserve the other half; they can prove what an agent did, not whether its findings survive the evidence. 

AI made analysis cheap, and in doing so made trust the scarce asset.  

Value is now in a layer where work is retained and compounds. AI analysis is retained and connected to source material with durable annotations, verified at write time, that survive hostile review and let the next analysis start from what's already known. 

## How it comes to know

A change arrives already annotated, section by section. An agent's finding is filed on the change — verified against the text it cites before it can enter the record. An analyst's disagreement is an annotation too, typed and anchored. A newer finding supersedes an older one, which stays in the record: retracted, still replayable.

That chain is the product: what your organization knows, and how it came to know it.

## Who it's for

Exeges is built for teams working in messy, changing, high-consequence environments — wherever the dataset is large, the ground shifts, and "show your work" is not optional.
